  Li Biao is a professor and doctoral advisor at the School of Architecture, Southeast University, and director of the Institute of Architectural Algorithms & Applications (Lab AAA). His research, teaching and practice focus on generative architectural design and the *digital chain* of integrated CNC fabrication.

  He holds an advanced master’s degree from ETH Zurich and a doctorate in architecture from Southeast University (nominated for the National Excellent Doctoral Dissertation in 2010), and was a postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich from 2009 to 2013. He joined the faculty of Southeast University in 1997 and became professor in 2010.

  He has carried algorithmic generation into built work: he was entrusted with the curtain-wall design for the international street of the Nanjing Youth Olympic Games, which won second prizes for excellent engineering design and excellent survey design in Jiangsu Province in 2016. Since 2015 he has coded and built digital structures including *Impression of Taihu Stone*, *Memory of Dragon Boat*, *Empty Shadow*, the Tangshan Quarry Park pedestrian bridge, and *Linpan* at Nan-an-mei village in Anren, Chengdu.

  He has published more than 40 papers in academic journals and conferences, led National Natural Science Foundation projects, participated in a key funded programme, and led a sub-project of the Ministry of Science and Technology’s 13th Five-Year National Key R&D Programme. He is the author of *Architectural Generative Design*, translator of *Beyond the Grid*, and editor of *Architecture · Computation · Application: Teaching and Research I*.

李飚，东南大学建筑学院教授、博士生导师，建筑运算与应用研究所（Lab AAA）所长。长期从事建筑数字生成设计、“数字链”一体化数控建造的理论研究、教学与实践。

他获瑞士联邦理工大学（ETH Zurich）高级硕士学位、东南大学建筑学博士学位（2010 年全国优秀博士论文提名），并于 2009—2013 年在 ETH 从事博士后研究。自 1997 年起任教于东南大学建筑学院，2010 年晋升教授。

他尝试将算法生成方法推向实际工程：获南京青奥会国际风情街幕墙设计权，该项目于 2016 年获江苏省优秀工程设计二等奖、优秀勘察设计二等奖。2015 年以来，他完成了印象太湖石、龙舟记忆、空影阑珊、汤山矿坑公园步行桥、成都安仁南岸美村“林·盘”等数字构筑物的编码与建造。

他在国内外学术期刊与会议发表论文四十余篇，主持国家自然科学基金项目、参与重点项目，并主持科技部“十三五”国家重点研发计划子课题；著有《建筑生成设计》，译有《超越网格》，主编《建筑·运算·应用：教学与研究Ⅰ》。
